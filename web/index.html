REF HOW TO READ THIS

Probabilities are the model's own scores, not measurement error bars, and not a statement about whether a planet exists. On CH-2 the service always shows what NASA's published rule says about the same numbers — when the model and the rule disagree, the rule is the reference and that disagreement is the interesting case. Extrapolated inputs are flagged rather than rejected. Full contracts at /docs, raw JSON at /api/v1/meta.
